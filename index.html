Re-evaluating RAPD-Based Plant DNA Evidence from the 1992 Maricopa Homicide Investigation: A Modern Reassessment of the First Court-Accepted Molecular Botanical Evidence

Laturisa J.L.

This study re-examines the Blue Palo Verde (Parkinsonia florida) seed pod evidence by reassessing its statistical weight using modern population genetics principles and evaluating its admissibility under the transition from the Frye to the Daubert standard.
